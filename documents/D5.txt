Natural Language Processing (NLP) is the study and development of systems designed to train computers to understand and interpret human language. By combining elements of linguistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence, NLP teaches machines to comprehend and respond to the inherent complexities of human communication. To achieve this, the field relies on three key components: syntax, which governs the grammatical structure of sentences; semantics, which focuses on the literal meaning of words and phrases; and pragmatics, which addresses how the meaning of words can vary based on specific contexts.
