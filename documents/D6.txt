Speech recognition, or speech-to-text, is the ability of a machine or program to identify words spoken aloud and convert them into readable text. Rudimentary speech recognition software has a limited vocabulary and might only identify words and phrases that are spoken clearly. More sophisticated software can handle natural speech, different accents and various languages. Speech recognition uses a broad array of research in computer science, linguistics and computer engineering. Many modern devices and text-focused programs have speech recognition functions in them to allow for easier or hands-free use of a device. They differ from text-to-speech systems, in which the system analyses text content and converts the text into spoken audio.
